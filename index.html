Good to Know

Questions,
Answered.

A few details before you apply.

Is this an open event?

No. This is a curated experience. Applying does not guarantee selection; invited couples will receive event details directly.

Who can apply?

Couples where both participants are 21 years or older. Please submit one application per couple.

What does the winner receive?

One winning couple receives a complimentary stay for two at Noir & Oak Stays, subject to availability and the written voucher terms shared with the winner.

Is the stay transferable or redeemable for cash?

The prize is intended for the winning couple, is not redeemable for cash, and is subject to the final event and voucher terms.

When will selected couples hear back?

Selected applicants will be contacted using the details provided in the application. Please check your email and phone messages.
